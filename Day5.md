# Day 5 - Chain of Thought Prompting

## Today's Learning

Today, I learned about Chain of Thought (CoT) Prompting.

Chain of Thought Prompting is a technique that encourages AI to think step by step before generating the final answer.

Instead of directly asking for an answer, we guide the AI to explain its reasoning process.

---

## What is Chain of Thought Prompting?

Chain of Thought Prompting helps AI break down complex problems into smaller steps and solve them logically.

This technique is especially useful for:

- Mathematical problems
- Logical reasoning
- Coding problems
- Decision-making tasks
- Interview preparation

---

## Example

### Normal Prompt

```
A shopkeeper buys a pen for ₹20 and sells it for ₹30. What is the profit?
```

### Chain of Thought Prompt

```
Solve the problem step by step.

A shopkeeper buys a pen for ₹20 and sells it for ₹30. What is the profit?
```

The AI first calculates:

- Cost Price = ₹20
- Selling Price = ₹30
- Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price
- Profit = ₹10

Final Answer: ₹10

---

## Benefits of Chain of Thought Prompting

- Improves reasoning ability.
- Produces more accurate responses.
- Reduces mistakes in complex problems.
- Makes AI explain the logic behind the answer.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Chain of Thought Prompting encourages step-by-step reasoning.
- It improves the quality and accuracy of responses.
- Useful for coding, mathematics, and analytical tasks.

---
